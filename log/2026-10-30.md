### Facts established for the design, without a MySQL server

- The emitted MySQL DDL gives `customers.code VARCHAR(128)` under `utf8mb4_0900_ai_ci` with
  `UNIQUE (code)`, `customers.name LONGTEXT`, `customers.active BIGINT`, `credit_profile.credit_object_type
  LONGTEXT`, `credit_profile.effective_from VARCHAR(128)` and `credit_profile.effective_to LONGTEXT`. The
  probe drove `bootstrapMySql` with a recording mock adapter; the probe script lives in the local
  temporary directory and the repository was not changed.
- `credit_object_type` is written through `requiredCode`, which bounds it to 50 characters, restricts it to
  `[A-Za-z0-9_-]` and uppercases it. The MySQL group-key truncation boundary of 1024 bytes is therefore
  unreachable for the supported range, and a case-only collision of that field is impossible. The
  `LONGTEXT` type alone does not prove a collision, and the short current fixture alone does not prove the
  supported range; the production writer contract settles both.
- The `UNIQUE (code)` key and the `PRIMARY KEY (id)` are themselves under the accent- and
  case-insensitive collation, so two identities that differ only by case or accent cannot be stored on
  MySQL at all. This is a storage-level environment difference and is classified as such, not as a
  business result.
- The free-text `customers.name` is the one dimension where the engines can genuinely diverge, because
  the SQLite `LOWER` folds ASCII only while the MySQL collation folds case and accents. The Requirement
  registers this as an open decision; the gate measures it and never asserts cross-engine equality on it.

  boundaries, but its customers are `id.toUpperCase()` with the name `id`, so it contains neither a
  mixed-case code nor an accent nor a second Credit Object nor an ambiguous effective version. The gate
  therefore needs its own dedicated fixture helper that extends the shared world without modifying the
  shared fixture files, so that the frozen U2 E3 suite keeps its exact data.
- The strict TAP contract of the protected campaign is exact: one `TAP version 13` header, exactly one
  top-level plan, contiguous unique ids, plain `ok` records, every summary field exactly once and zero
  fail, cancelled, skipped and todo. The new suite must therefore be flat top-level with ten tests.
