# V1.3 Phase 5 — AR/AP Open-item and Settlement Integrity

## Canonical model

Every confirmed Sales Delivery creates exactly one positive `account_receivables` source item; every confirmed Purchase Receipt creates exactly one positive `account_payables` source item. The existing `(source_type, source_id)` unique indexes prevent duplicate sources. New returns and discounts no longer create detached negative AR/AP rows.

Canonical history consists of:

- `financial_credit_adjustments`: immutable return/discount credits linked to the originating positive AR/AP item;
- confirmed allocation rows in `payment_collection_items` / `payment_disbursement_items`, net of `reversed_cents`;
- the existing controlled `write_off_cents` field (no ordinary Phase 5 UI writes it).

`paid_cents`, credit subtotal columns, `open_amount_cents`, and `status` on AR/AP are derived caches. Normal writes refresh them in the same transaction. Startup does not silently repair them. `GET /api/settlement/reconciliation` is CHECK-only and reports cache discrepancies without mutation.

The equations are:

```text
AR open = original - applied return credit - applied sales discount
           - other controlled credit - confirmed collection allocations
           + allocation reversals - write-off

AP open = original - applied purchase-return credit - applied purchase discount
           - other controlled credit - confirmed payment allocations
           + allocation reversals - write-off
```

Open is never negative. `OPEN`, `PARTIAL`, and `SETTLED` are derived from source-level activity, never from a party aggregate.

## Document contracts

| Document | Source / role | Open-item effect | Cash/bank | Voucher | Reversal / delete |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sales Delivery | Approved SO; warehouse confirms after OQC PASS | Creates one AR source | None | One source-unique delivery voucher | Confirmed immutable |
| Sales Return | Confirmed Delivery; warehouse confirms | Linked return credit applies to its Delivery AR; excess is customer credit | None | One source-unique reversal voucher | Confirmed immutable; operational return reversal remains outside Phase 5 |
| Sales Discount | Accounting targets an eligible AR | Linked discount credit; excess is customer credit | None | One source-unique voucher | Explicit discount reversal; confirmed hard delete refused |
| Collection | Accounting creates/confirms | Confirmed allocations reduce selected AR items | The existing settlement path has no separate bank-balance mutation; voucher is the canonical treasury accounting effect | One source-unique voucher | Full-only linked reversal; draft/cancelled zero-effect document may be deleted |
| Collection Reversal | Accounting; current open period date | Reverses every original allocation and restores AR open | Exact opposite accounting effect | Equal-and-opposite source-unique voucher | Cannot repeat |
| Purchase Receipt | Approved PO; warehouse confirms after IQC PASS | Creates one AP source | None | One source-unique receipt voucher | Confirmed immutable |
| Purchase Return | Confirmed Receipt; warehouse confirms | Linked return credit applies to its Receipt AP; excess is supplier credit | None | One source-unique reversal voucher | Confirmed immutable; operational return reversal remains outside Phase 5 |
| Purchase Discount | Accounting targets an eligible AP | Linked discount credit; excess is supplier credit | None | One source-unique voucher | Explicit discount reversal; confirmed hard delete refused |
| Payment | Accounting creates/confirms | Confirmed allocations reduce selected AP items | Voucher is the canonical treasury accounting effect | One source-unique voucher | Full-only linked reversal; draft/cancelled zero-effect document may be deleted |
| Payment Reversal | Accounting; current open period date | Reverses every original allocation and restores AP open | Exact opposite accounting effect | Equal-and-opposite source-unique voucher | Cannot repeat |

Settlement confirmation re-reads the draft and every selected source inside one SQLite transaction, recomputes current open, validates party ownership and caps, requires allocation sum to equal the header amount, creates the voucher, confirms the document, refreshes caches, and audits. Any failure rolls everything back. Unallocated collection/payment and overpayment are refused.

## Terms, due dates, and aging

Customer and supplier masters carry `payment_terms_days >= 0`. SO and PO copy this numeric value as an immutable commercial snapshot; descriptive `payment_terms` remains display text and is never parsed. Delivery AR due date is Delivery business date plus the SO snapshot. Receipt AP due date is Receipt business date plus the PO snapshot. Legacy rows retain their existing date and nullable/unknown numeric terms. Lists expose due date, overdue days, and the Not due / 1–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+ buckets.

## Credits after settlement

A credit first applies to its authoritative source up to that source's current open amount. The remainder is stored as explicit `unapplied_cents`. It is included in customer/supplier net position but is not silently allocated to another or future source. Thus a fully settled AR followed by a 2,000 credit remains at zero open and produces a 2,000 customer credit.

Customer balance is derived as total positive source AR open minus confirmed unapplied customer credits. Supplier balance is symmetric. Neither master record stores an independently mutable balance.

## Closed periods and uniqueness

All confirmations and reversals use the shared accounting-period guard in voucher generation. A historical entry is never rewritten; reversal uses the chosen current open-period business date. Unsafe posting into a closed period is refused atomically.

`accounting_vouchers(source_type, source_id)` is unique. Operational confirmation retries therefore cannot create duplicate GL effects. The five canonical roles and five Approval Center families are unchanged; settlement documents and discounts are not Approval Center families. Accounting owns the ordinary workflow, while the manual-voucher creator/approver separation remains unchanged.

## Legacy compatibility

Existing negative v1.2/M14 AR/AP rows are retained read-only as `LEGACY`; no source link is fabricated. New Phase 5 writes use only positive `SOURCE` items plus linked credit history. The one-time migration initializes derived caches for pre-existing positive Delivery/Receipt rows and records a migration marker. Later application startup performs no silent cache repair.

## Phase boundary

Deferred to Phase 6: credit allocation/refund/prepayment workflows, partial settlement reversal, operational return reversal, advanced reopen policy, and other controlled write-off UI. Deferred to Phase 7: MySQL migration, true database concurrency validation, upgrade/rollback, and release engineering.
